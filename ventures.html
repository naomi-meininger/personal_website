Teaching

Bright Young Women: Feminism and Gender Representation in Disney

Tufts ExCollege

The ExCollege lets undergraduates design and teach a class on a topic of their choosing. I've always been fascinated by Disney's complicated legacy, and the course grew out of a line in The Little Mermaid's "Part of Your World": Ariel sings about being ready to stand on her own, yet the film's plot pulls in a different direction. That tension got me thinking about the roles Ariel and the other Disney princesses really play in their films.

A highlight was bringing in Susan Egan, the voice of Meg in Hercules, to speak to the class.
